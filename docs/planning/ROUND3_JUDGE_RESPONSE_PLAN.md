## Single outcome to evaluate

Given a declared campus topology, configured demand and an outage, recommend the best permitted recovery within the supported modeled electrical limits, explain its effect on critical appliances, and reproduce the decision and its tradeoffs. Real geography does not establish real electrical assets. Passing simulation tests does not establish field safety, campus ML accuracy, customer demand or ROI.

The three issues below cover all ten judges. They organize existing work rather than replace the required lab classifier, change the six-service catalog, resume paused hardware, or add RL as a prerequisite. Scope/profile decisions must be explicit; the legacy 14 kW campus and 6 kW district are not silently merged. No score or positive benchmark outcome is guaranteed.

## Workstream 2 — GitHub #70

### Outcome and priority
**P0 · Round 3, workstream 2 of 3.** Recommend the best permitted recovery for a bounded configured campus case, and refuse proposals that improve served watts while violating declared electrical limits. Show the actual calculation and its assumptions. “Validated” means the specified simulation checks passed, never proof of operational grid safety.

Plan baseline: `87a9c11f844e4c2f4b4866292897ada7de0d3772`, 2026-10-10; responds to the supplied review of `e55f4a0`.

### Current gap
`DistrictAuthority.apply_action()` selects the first tie that improves service. `_electrical_state()` clips requests to 6,000 W/source and edge W limits; that is no AC voltage/current/loss check. `advance_hour` increments stability evidence. The separate optional `backend/app/simulation/electrical.py` solves a fixed balanced two-feeder study and intentionally cannot authorize restoration. Reuse its engine/validation patterns, not its fixed topology or any safety implication. ### Commit-sized implementation sequence
1. **Freeze the engineering contract and reference cases.** Consume [#69](https://github.com/Sanjith-Jam/blackout-mesh/issues/69)'s configured graph/load identities. Explicitly specify voltage base (line-line versus line-neutral), phases, frequency, length and R/X units, transformer ratio/vector group/impedance/kVA, source/slack rating and short-circuit assumption, power factor or P/Q, load type, switch states, PV/storage connection and SOC. Serialize required parameters from the generator/catalog; do not reconstruct missing impedance from geometry or treat VA as W. Document synthetic parameter ranges and cited primary equipment assumptions; missing/incompatible ratings block validation. Publish the rated-power conversions and conservative integer-W rounding rules with tests.
2. **Adapt the existing optional AC engine to the configured graph.** Keep one authority and execute bounded studies away from publication/heartbeats. Check connectivity first, then convergence, voltage range, branch ampacity, transformer apparent-power loading, source P/Q and real-power losses/balance with predeclared tolerances. Include losses in the source budget. Isolated quantities remain null. Prohibit incompatible-voltage ties and unsourced islands; behind-meter assets cannot energize a dead feeder unless an explicitly supported island/slack model exists. Do not equate numerical convergence with an acceptable operating point.
3. **Add explicit asymmetric validation.** Start with a balanced regression fixture, then validate the demo's final candidate on a supported unbalanced three-phase model with specified per-phase loads and neutral/grounding assumptions. Exercise one phase overload/asymmetric undervoltage that an aggregate-W check misses. Use the installed engine only if it supports the necessary model; otherwise return UNSUPPORTED and keep that candidate advisory. Protection coordination, inrush, transient/dynamic stability and real switch interlocks remain outside this steady-state model; expose those missing checks and never label the result safe for autonomous physical restoration.
4. **Replace first-benefit selection with a bounded defined optimization.** For the small demo, exhaustively evaluate declared permitted switch configurations and mapped load decisions with explicit limits, reusing CP-SAT for appliance decisions. Define a lexicographic objective before measurement: maximize served critical W, then configured weighted served W, then minimize switching actions, with stable ID tie-break. Define binary appliance versus divisible aggregate-load semantics. Enforce known-open/faulted paths, radial energized components, voltage compatibility, source/line/transformer limits and permitted operations. For each configuration, independently validate the proposed load decision using AC power flow; a failed AC check may require another load decision, so one greedy/CP-SAT incumbent per topology is not a proof of global optimality. Claim OPTIMAL only when the entire declared feasible decision domain is exhausted/proven; otherwise report FEASIBLE, incumbent/bound/gap where available, and truncation/timeout. Do not force an unjustified global-optimum claim for a large district.
5. **Make proposals immutable and apply only current validated results.** Attach topology/profile/observation digests, run/revision, candidate switch/load set, objective components, solver status and AC checks. Keep candidate evaluation pure; do not temporarily mutate live `closed_tie` while solving. Revalidate current evidence and capacities before modeled application. If optional engine/parameters are absent, solver fails/times out, revision changes, or a necessary check is unknown, refuse validated application with an explicit reason. Legacy watt-budget demonstrations remain labeled accordingly; no physical commands originate from this path.
6. **Replace action-count restoration evidence.** Ingest timestamped/sequence-numbered causal observations and explicit observed switch status. Distinct fresh healthy samples over a configured dwell interval support restoration; advancing an hour or reading the same sample twice does not. Reset the gate on unhealthy/stale/missing/reordered evidence, topology/capacity change or restart. Protective isolation/shedding stays immediate. The fault injector is separate simulator input: tests prove hidden fault labels/scenario IDs cannot influence the planner/diagnoser without an observable change. A demo observation adapter may expose simulated open-switch evidence, labeled as such; do not claim independent fault localization.
7. **Build the oracle, stress suite and explanations.** Deliver #65 here. Add small independent exhaustive decision fixtures and multiple-fault/multiple-tie graphs, including one where first-benefit loses, a loop, no benefit, unreachable load, capacity reduction, stale apply and no feasible recovery. Include an apparently useful tie rejected for undervoltage/current/transformer loading with V/A/VA limits, engine/version, residuals and input digest visible. Check one accepted and one rejected case with the existing offline pandapower cross-engine path and declared tolerances. Reject invalid intermediate switching sequences, not only final graphs; stay within the allowed break-before-make operations.
